# EP06: "Why Is Pune Drumming at 9 PM?"
**Pune Rule #6:** *Two months before Ganeshotsav, Pune finds its rhythm.*
**Runtime:** 60 s · **Cast:** Bablu, Rohan, Aaji, Arjun · **Shape:** a mystery → a performance payoff
**Tone:** respectful and celebratory. The comedy comes from tired Rohan's double life, never from the festival or the deity.

## Logline
Every night, Arjun hears thunderous drumming and assumes it's a protest. Bablu takes him to find out, and they discover tired IT-guy Rohan is secretly the lead dhol player of a pathak (a drum troupe).

## Hook (0–2 s)
A black screen. **BOOM. BOOM-BOOM.** The window glass vibrates and a cup of tea ripples (a nod to a classic suspense shot).
**BABLU:** "Why are two hundred people drumming at 9 pm? Let me show you."
