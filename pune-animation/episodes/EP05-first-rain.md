# EP05: "First Rain in Pune"
**Pune Rule #5:** *When the first rain falls, all plans are cancelled. Bhaji is mandatory.*
**Runtime:** 58 s · **Cast:** Bablu, Rohan, Meera, Aaji, Arjun · **Shape:** montage → tender ending (the most shareable "heart" episode)

## Logline
The first monsoon shower hits Pune. Everyone drops what they're doing: Rohan quits a meeting, Meera abandons her exam revision, Aaji brings out the bhaji pan, and they all end up on Sinhagad in the mist.

## Hook (0–2 s)
A single raindrop falls in slow motion and lands on Bablu's eye. He blinks. Silence. Then: **THUNDER.**
**BABLU:** "POV: it's the first rain in Pune… and everyone has lost their minds."
