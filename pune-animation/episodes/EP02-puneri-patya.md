# EP02: "Aaji's Signboards" (Puneri Patya)
**Pune Rule #2:** *In Pune, the board outside is ruder than the owner inside.*
**Runtime:** 55 s · **Cast:** Bablu, Aaji, Arjun, Pinya · **Shape:** comic escalation → reveal

## Logline
Arjun returns Aaji's charger and gets "insulted" by three increasingly savage signboards on the way to her door. Then he finds out who writes them.

## Hook (0–2 s)

**BABLU (VO):** "If you can read this sign… you're already insulted."
