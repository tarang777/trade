# Series bible: "Pune Diaries: Rides with Bablu"

## 1. Premise
Bablu is a 20-year-old yellow-and-black Pune auto-rickshaw with a mind of his own. He has seen the city change from cycles to the metro. Every episode is one ride and one Pune "rule". Bablu narrates directly to the camera like a cheeky tour guide.

## 2. Main cast

### BABLU (narrator, the auto-rickshaw)
- **Look:** a classic Indian three-wheeler auto, **black lower body, yellow canopy**. The **headlight is his single big expressive eye** (eyelid = a little visor). The front grille is his mouth. A small orange marigold garland on the mirror and a dented left mudguard ("from 2009, don't ask"). A **hand-painted sign on the back reads "Horn OK Please… pan halu"** (*but gently*).
- **Personality:** a street-smart, sarcastic, secretly soft-hearted local. He knows every shortcut. He is proud of old Pune and slightly scared of Hinjewadi traffic.
- **Voice:** a warm male voice in his late 30s, fast Hinglish with Marathi words.
- **Catchphrases:** "*Kay re!* Bablu here. Seat belt nahi hai, *pan* hold on." / "That's Pune Rule number ___." / "*Ekdum bhari!*"

### AAJI (Shalini Kulkarni, 72)
- **Look:** a nine-yard **nauvari saree** (maroon with a green border), round gold spectacles, silver hair in a bun, a cloth shopping bag, a tiny umbrella in every season.
- **Personality:** the queen of the Sadashiv Peth wada. She bargains like a pro, writes Puneri Patya signs for fun, and secretly watches K-dramas.
- **Voice:** sharp and crisp, with a pure Puneri Marathi accent.

### ROHAN (Rohan Deshpande, 27)
- **Look:** a software engineer. A grey hoodie over a formal shirt, an ID lanyard, a laptop backpack, earbuds, and permanent dark circles.
- **Personality:** a Pune-born IT guy whose life is the commute to Hinjewadi. Optimistic every morning, defeated by 10 am.
- **Voice:** a tired millennial, English-heavy Hinglish.

### MEERA (Meera Shaikh, 20)
- **Look:** a college student. A yellow kurta over jeans, a canvas tote covered in pins, a scooter helmet with stickers, curly hair in a scrunchie.
- **Personality:** a food vlogger wannabe and FC Road regular who rates every misal out of 10.
- **Voice:** an energetic Gen-Z voice.

### "NEW GUY" ARJUN (Arjun Nair, 24): a recurring outsider
- **Look:** just moved from Kochi for a job. Carries a phone with maps open and is always slightly lost.
- **Function:** the **audience surrogate**. Bablu explains Pune to him.

### Minor recurring
- **Pinya the pigeon:** lives on Bablu's canopy and reacts silently (a cutaway gag).
- **Chai-stall Kaka:** the philosopher of the tea stall. One-line wisdom per episode.

## 4. Visual style (Track B master look)
- **Style:** a stylised 3D animated-film look. Soft, warm, Pixar-adjacent proportions with hand-painted textures. Indian colour palette.
- **Palette:** marigold yellow, auto-rickshaw black, laterite red stone, monsoon green, dusk saffron, sky teal.
- **Lighting:** golden hour for warm scenes, soft overcast for the monsoon, neon and sodium light for night.
- **Camera:** mostly eye-level from Bablu's height, quick push-ins on punchlines, top-down shots for the traffic gags.
- **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical. Keep characters in the **centre 60%** (caption-safe).
- **Track A (code-drawn)** alternative look: flat 2D paper-cut style, bold outlines, the same palette.

## 5. Tone rules
1. Laugh with Pune, never at it.
2. Every episode ends on **heart** (a warm or nostalgic line).
3. No villains, only situations (traffic, rain, closing times).
4. Use 2–3 Marathi words per episode max, always captioned.
5. Never make fun of religion, caste, community or accents of non-Marathi speakers.

## 7. Recurring gags (for series memory)
- **The Pune Rule counter**: every episode reveals one numbered rule, shown as a hand-painted sign.
- **Pinya the pigeon** reacts to every punchline (a 1-second cutaway).
- **Bablu's dent**: "from 2009, don't ask". The cause is revealed in the season finale.
- **Aaji's patya**: Aaji writes a new sign every episode and it appears in the background of later episodes.
- **The ending**: Bablu's meter clicks over and he says "*Meter down!* Next ride, soon."
